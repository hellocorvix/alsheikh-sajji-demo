A Sabzazar institution

At Alsheikh, we practice the ancient Balochi craft of sajji — whole birds marinated in secret spices, skewered, and slow-roasted around open coals until the skin crisps and the meat falls from the bone. This isn't fast food. This is tradition, fire, and time.

Open every evening from 6 PM to 2 AM, we serve only what's been prepared fresh that night. The coals are lit, the flames lick the air, and Lahoris gather for what many call the best sajji in the city. Come hungry. Come late. Come often.
